# Ava Advisor

**Financial intelligence. Investment simplified.**

Ava Advisor ([ava.fund](https://ava.fund)) is a personal investment advisor for everybody, built by
Market Anyware Co., Ltd. in Thailand.

## Why we exist
Technology moved investing from trading floors to computers to phones, but investors still spend hours a day staring at
charts, prices, news and financial statements. By our own rough estimate, that's around 50 million hours a day worldwide.
Our goal is to give that time back to the people investors care about.

## What Ava Advisor does
- **A screener you talk to.** Screen stocks in plain words, for example "biotech stocks with D/E below 3, turning
  profitable this year, showing a bullish divergence", across fundamentals, technicals and news.
- **Personal alerts.** Get alerted on what matters to *you*, not on everything.
- **Why markets move.** We map financial news to the stocks, themes and assets it actually affects: the direction, the reason
  and the ripple to connected companies.
- **Data you can trust.** AI agents validate, cross-check and update financials and news continuously.
- **A financial knowledge graph.** Themes, GICS sectors, sentiment, peers and competitors, all linked.
- **Built for your AI.** Connect any MCP-compatible assistant to get this structured data inside the chat.
